Sleep 8 Pain Relief Balm — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about applying Sleep 8 Balm for muscle discomfort, neck, shoulder, and knee stiffness.

1. What is Sleep 8 Pain Relief Balm used for?

Sleep 8 Pain Relief Balm is formulated for external application to help provide temporary relief from minor aches, muscle discomfort and stiffness.

2. How do I apply Sleep 8 Pain Relief Balm?

Take a small amount of balm with your fingertip and gently massage it onto the affected area for 2–3 minutes.

3. Can I use Sleep 8 Balm for back and neck discomfort?

Yes. It can be gently applied to areas such as the back, neck and shoulders experiencing minor aches or discomfort.

4. Can it be used for knee and muscle discomfort?

Yes. The balm can be applied externally to the knees and areas experiencing minor muscle discomfort.

5. Is Sleep 8 Pain Relief Balm for external use only?

Yes. It is intended for external application only. Avoid contact with the eyes, mouth and sensitive or broken skin.

6. How often can I use Sleep 8 Pain Relief Balm?

Use the balm as directed on the product packaging. Do not exceed the recommended amount or frequency.

7. Can everyone use Sleep 8 Pain Relief Balm?

If you have sensitive skin, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are using other medicines, consult a healthcare professional before use.

8. What should I do if I experience skin irritation?

Stop using the product if irritation or an allergic reaction occurs. If symptoms persist or become severe, seek medical advice.
